Hi, I'm Audrey! I'm a senior at Princeton majoring in Electrical Engineering with a concentration in optics and photonics and a side interest in comms. I'm currently working in Princeton's Lightwave Communications Research Lab under Paul Prucnal.

There's a lot I love outside of engineering too! I love to learn more about the world, and I think it's important to do so through many different domains. My favorite way to learn is through my hobbies: art, figure skating, hiking, reading, running, and travelling!

I firmly believe that the best engineering is people-centric. If you also love talking engineering, physics, art, books, or anything else nerdy, reach out to me! I love new friends :)

Classes

Spring '26: Embedded Systems

Designed two mixed-signal PCBs for control of a remote car. Both powered from 9.6V batteries with RF communication capabilities. Controller board sends motor control commands to the car depending on IMU data. Both drive custom-programmed LED strips. Compute done on a custom-programmed STM32 MCU.

Fall '25: Computer Architecture

Simulated a multicycle CPU for the RISC-V ISA in C++, which executed RISC-V programs and gave performance statistics. Included a custom cache, 5-stage pipelining and full forwarding and stalls.

Fall '25: Robotic and Autonomous Systems Lab

Built a fully autonomous car with a glove for remote control. Designed and fabricated a power distribution and motor driver board, a sensing board for speed control, an analog video demodulation board, and a flex sensor glove circuit. Wrote all of our own drivers in embedded C for a PSOC MCU. Wrote BLE firmware for communication between the glove and the car.

Fall '24: Electronic and Photonic Devices

Fabricated, tested, and characterized a chip on a silicon wafer with n-channel MOSFETs, photodiodes, resistor arrays, solar cells, ring oscillators, and NAND/NOR gates.
